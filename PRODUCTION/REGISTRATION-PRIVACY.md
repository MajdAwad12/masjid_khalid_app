# Registration privacy and release checks

## Staff procedure

A valid new submission reserves one available seat immediately. Approval-mode programs record a pending seat and automatic programs record an accepted seat. Duplicate, full-program and installation-limit submissions receive the same acknowledgment and leave the roster unchanged. Staff see live counts in private documents. Public submissions leave historical totals untouched. A staff seat change or reconciliation clears those obsolete fields, so older clients can submit again when space opens. Existing hourly installation and network limits still apply. Until staff verify the installation, its active seats per program are capped at eight for courses and ten for activities, across hourly windows.

1. Contact the participant or authorized representative through an independently trusted channel. Establish authority for this participant and program using the publisher’s approved procedure. A matching name, phone number, or device reference alone is insufficient.
2. Ask the authorized person to open **تسجيلاتي** in the relevant section on the originating installation and read its reference. From the individual roster row choose **التحقق من المشارك والجهاز**, enter the reference, and confirm the identity check. Each row requires verification, including legacy rows. This records the staff UID and time. It does not certify legal guardian consent.
3. Verification grants access to the individual status on the originating installation. It preserves an existing seat and decision even if enrollment has closed. Historical requests still marked unverified follow the original availability check on verification. Staff can approve or reject a reserved request before verifying the installation. Approval and rejection send a generic Arabic update when the installation has an eligible push token. Keep the independent identity check for withdrawal and erasure.
4. For an authorized withdrawal, choose **تسجيل الانسحاب**. It releases an occupied seat and keeps the record. Rejection also keeps a record. For an authorized erasure, choose **محو بيانات التسجيل**. Confirm the precise participant and program. This removes the single live document, including its name, phone, decision, installation hash and FCM token. A delayed repeat cannot erase a newer registration with the same identifier. Other records for that participant require separate verification and selection.
5. Help a claimant whose original submission came from the wrong device through the independent identity procedure. Staff can erase an incorrect record and arrange a fresh submission on the correct installation. Do not ask anyone to send a password, reset link, raw installation key, or FCM token.

## Retention decisions for the publisher

| Records | Implemented behavior | Decision outstanding |
|---|---|---|
| Unverified, pending, accepted, rejected and withdrawn registrations | Individual verified erasure or retryable cascade after deleting the parent. Closing or hiding a program preserves the records. | Pending seats have no automatic expiry. Decide when staff should review abandoned reservations and whether a future expiry policy is appropriate. Set retention by purpose after program end and for abandoned requests. Assign the person responsible for requests and response times. |
| Installation credentials, FCM tokens, and delivery metadata | The mobile installation keeps its existing local key. Registrations store a hash, an optional push token and delivery state. Confirmed invalid tokens are cleared from processed records. Individual erasure removes that record’s credentials and delivery state. | Set token inactivity limits, review shared-device and backup policy, and decide how to process installations that never return. |
| Staff identities, recovery email and verification/reset records | The existing self-deletion worker removes Auth and associated recovery records. Expiration checks limit token use. Published content and staff attribution can survive account deletion. | Approve attribution, expired-record cleanup and any operational retention exceptions. |
| Rate limits, operational logs, backups, restore copies and public delivery ledger | App-level expiry checks and delivery deduplication do not prove infrastructure deletion. | Confirm actual IAM, log destinations, enabled TTL policies, backup windows, restore procedures and access. Approve schedules before enabling deletion. |
| Minors and religious-program participation | Forms remind public and staff submitters of third-party and guardian permission. Verification is specific to identity and installation. | Approve audience, territories, guardian authority and evidence, media rights, and whether attendance is used to infer religious belief. Collect only evidence necessary for the approved process. |

## Evidence for the store forms

The app collects contact and participation data and links registration decisions to a named person and installation. Optional staff recovery email, staff identifiers, published media and text, installation and notification identifiers, and service diagnostics also need consideration. An activity or course name and participant name are excluded from private push payloads. A public news alert still contains public content. Firebase processes service metadata. Shorebird patch-enabled builds also communicate per-installation update information.

Complete Google Play Data Safety and Apple App Privacy using the shipped SDKs, actual processor contracts, purpose, linkage, and audience. The publisher must confirm any sharing exception, tracking answer, security assurance, or sensitive-data classification. Review the [Google Play definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469), [Apple definitions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/), [Firebase Android disclosures](https://firebase.google.com/docs/android/play-data-disclosure), [Firebase Apple disclosures](https://firebase.google.com/docs/ios/app-store-data-collection), and [Shorebird collection](https://docs.shorebird.dev/system/what-shorebird-collects/).

## Verification outside the local suite

- On shared Android and iOS devices, exercise sign-out, revoked staff access with a roster open, offline transitions, process restart, and backup/restore. Staff roster views require a server-confirmed role and snapshot. This UI protection does not erase historical Firestore disk caches, OS backups, screenshots, or another person’s access to a shared unlocked installation.
- Test FCM/APNs token rotation, opt-out, lock-screen previews and delivery after interruption on physical devices. Known completed sends are suppressed. Verify the course and activity decision-recovery triggers can resume pending work if the callable is interrupted. When FCM acceptance is uncertain, the app retains verified in-app status as the fallback. A provider acknowledgment does not guarantee display.
- Before enabling the new client, verify the deployed Functions, existing rules, actual peer IP handling, queries/indexes, event retries and service permissions with designated synthetic records. Older Functions will not enforce verification. Coordinate backend, compatible clients and legal hosting. Verify the rendered privacy, terms, support and deletion URLs after publication and exercise the monitored support route.
- Check production backup and log settings separately. A live record deletion does not purge existing backups or mail already sent. Review receipts, media download links, and retained staff attribution before completing the store declarations.
